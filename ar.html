QUICK HELP

Getting the best alignment

Hold the iPhone upright in portrait mode and point it toward the horizon. Tap Calibrate, then slowly pan left or right.

Compass readings can drift near cars, fences, speakers and magnetic phone mounts. Move a few metres away and calibrate again when labels look offset.

Aircraft positions may be delayed, missing or inaccurate. This is a fun spotting aid only—not a navigation or safety system.
