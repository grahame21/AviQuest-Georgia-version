AVIQUEST AR RECOVERY v5

FIXES
- Aircraft labels no longer disappear when iPhone blocks compass access.
- The nearest three aircraft appear in a centred fallback stack until compass access works.
- Calibrate retries Motion & Orientation access.
- Camera video is forced above the dark app background.
- Safari's previous video stream is cleared before attaching the new stream.
- Camera automatically retries if no usable frame appears after startup.
- The dark overlay is lighter.
- Vertical movement: phone up moves labels up; phone down moves labels down.
- Origin and destination route enrichment from v4 remains included.

INSTALL
Upload everything inside AviQuest-Georgia-version-main to the repository root.
Wait for Netlify to deploy.
Close the old Safari tab completely, then reopen the Netlify URL.
